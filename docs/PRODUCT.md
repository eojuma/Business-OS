# Executive Summary

Business OS is an AI-powered operating system designed specifically for hardware stores in Africa.

Instead of relying on notebooks, spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages, and memory, business owners can manage inventory, sales, expenses, suppliers, customers, and financial performance from one intelligent platform.

The system uses AI to simplify daily operations by allowing users to interact naturally through text or voice while automatically updating business records and generating actionable insights.

---

# Problem Statement

Millions of small and medium-sized businesses across Africa operate without reliable digital management systems.

Hardware stores face several operational challenges including:

- Poor inventory tracking
- Manual bookkeeping
- Unmanaged customer credit
- Stock shortages
- Poor supplier management
- Inaccurate financial reporting
- Lack of business insights
- Time-consuming administrative work

Most existing software is either:

- Too expensive
- Too complicated
- Designed for large enterprises
- Not tailored for African business workflows

# Why This Project Exists

Running a hardware store should not require managing multiple notebooks, spreadsheets, calculators, and WhatsApp chats.

Business owners deserve intelligent software that works the way they already communicate.

Instead of learning complicated software, users simply tell the system what happened.

Example:

"I sold 15 bags of cement."

"I bought stock worth KSh 80,000."

"How much profit did I make this month?"

Business OS performs the rest automatically.

# AI Features

Business OS includes an intelligent AI assistant capable of:

- Recording transactions using natural language
- Answering business questions
- Generating reports
- Identifying slow-moving products
- Predicting stock shortages
- Summarizing daily business performance
- Recommending products to reorder
- Explaining profit and loss
- Detecting unusual spending patterns

Example interactions:

> Sold 10 hammers for KSh 3,500.

> Which customers owe me more than KSh 20,000?

> Which products made the highest profit this month?

> Which products haven't sold in the last 60 days?
